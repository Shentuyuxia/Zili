Privacy

字里 · Zili is a private, non-commercial learning tool. Last updated: November 2026.

By default, everything stays on your device

Without signing in, your progress, your own words, sentences, recordings and “My articles” are stored only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

If you sign in with email to sync

  • What is stored: your email address and your sync data (progress, your own words, sentences, read marks, and “My articles” if you choose). No name, phone number or location.
  • Where: Supabase servers in Dublin, Ireland (EU).
  • Who can read it: only you, through the app. The maintainer can technically access the database; with “Encrypt my data” on, data is encrypted on your device before upload, so neither the maintainer nor the provider can read it.
  • Email: used only to send login codes.
  • Deleting: “Delete account and cloud data” in Settings → Sync removes your account and cloud data immediately. Data on your device stays.

Outside services

  • Speech input (microphone in the dictionary): handled by your browser’s own speech recognition (Apple on iPhone, Google in Chrome), only while the microphone is on. Handwriting and photo recognition run on your device; photos are not uploaded.
  • Wiktionary: lookups use the built-in offline dictionary (CC-CEDICT, HanDeDict); only words it lacks are sent to Wiktionary.
  • Hugging Face: only if you download an open-source neural voice, the voice model comes from Hugging Face. The text being read is processed on your device.
  • Claude (Anthropic): only if you add your own API key. The key stays on this device; the words you choose to look up are sent to api.anthropic.com.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app and the daily texts.

Fonts and libraries are self-hosted; nothing is loaded from Google or other third parties.

隐私说明（中文）

字里 · Zili 是一个私人维护、非商业的学习工具。最后更新：2026 年 10 月

默认：一切都只在你的设备上

不登录的话，你的学习进度、自录单词、例句、录音和「我的文章」只保存在这台设备的浏览器里。我们看不到，也不会上传。没有广告，没有统计或追踪代码。

开启邮箱登录同步时

  • 存什么：你的邮箱地址，以及同步数据（学习进度、自录单词、例句、读过的文章标记；如果你勾选，还有「我的文章」）。不收集姓名、手机号或位置。
  • 存在哪里：Supabase 的欧盟服务器（爱尔兰都柏林）。
  • 谁能看到：只有你能通过 app 读写自己的数据。网站维护者在技术上可以访问数据库；如果你开启「加密我的数据」，数据在你的设备上加密后才上传，维护者和服务商都只能看到密文。
  • 邮件：只用来发送登录验证码，不发其他邮件。
  • 删除：在「设置 → 多设备同步」里点「删除账号和云端数据」，账号和云端数据会立刻永久删除。设备上的数据不受影响。

会连接的外部服务

  • 语音输入（字典里的麦克风按钮）：由浏览器自带的语音识别处理，iPhone 上交给苹果、Chrome 里交给谷歌。只有你按下麦克风时才会录音。手写识别和拍照识字都完全在设备上进行，照片不会上传。
  • Wiktionary（维基媒体）：查词先用本站自带的离线词典（CC-CEDICT、HanDeDict）；只有离线词典里没有的词，才会发给 Wiktionary。
  • Hugging Face：只有你在设置里下载开源神经语音时，才会从 Hugging Face 下载语音模型。朗读的文字在你的设备上处理，不会上传。
  • GitHub：app 本身和每日文章放在 GitHub Pages 上。

字体和程序组件都放在本站，不会从谷歌等第三方加载。
